Battle readout & history

The readout and its history on their own, built by the same createBattleReadout and fed by the same createBattleLog the game uses — so the long-history scenarios below behave exactly as a real game's would, without playing one.

The readout at the top of a game shows every die when the fight is small enough to be worth it — four a side or fewer — and falls back to the compact reading past that, or whenever the full one would not fit the width it has. A history row is always compact, whatever room it has.
