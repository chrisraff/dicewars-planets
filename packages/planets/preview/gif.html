A match as a GIF

A real match played by the real AI, one camera, one frame per turn, and no roll animation — the planet changing colour is the whole of it. A Dicewars board only means anything as a sequence, so this is the one way to show somebody the game without asking them to play it.

A match is not a fixed length: two experts six-handed can grind for a hundred rounds while four of them settle it in twelve. So turns is a knob — take the first however many and stop. The preview plays at the rate the file will.
