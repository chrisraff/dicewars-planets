Replay storage & timing

What it costs to keep a match's replay in its save. Real matches are played out here and then put through the real serializeReplay, writeSavedGame and readSavedGame — against real localStorage, under a key of this page's own, so a game in progress in another tab is never touched. The interesting row is snapshot + stringify plus localStorage.setItem: together they are what every single move a player makes has to pay for, since a save is written on every change.
