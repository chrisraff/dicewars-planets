Moon

The second world, and the timetable that reaches it. The moon is constructed rather than carved and measured, because it spins: a different territory faces the planet at every stop, and the whole balance of the mode rests on whatever it presents being cheap to land on. So the garrisons ramp by latitude on the spin axis — an equatorial band that is the only ground the spin ever shows, and polar caps behind it that never face the planet at all and hold the dice worth taking.
