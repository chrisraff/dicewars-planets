First-turn prompt

The one-off panel that tells somebody who has never played what to actually do with the planet. In a real game it appears once and is remembered in localStorage, which is exactly why it is here: every state below is a real createHud running the real attackHintView, so you can look at it without clearing anything. The × works — press it and the log under the stage says so; "Show it again" puts it back.
