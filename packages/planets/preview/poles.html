Pole markers

The reference at each pole, on a real planet with real dice — a cone of fog in glass, additive and unlit, so it brightens what is behind it rather than having a surface of its own to catch a highlight. Drag to turn the planet: the marker should come up strongly as you swing over a pole and fall back to almost nothing side-on. Every slider is live, and the readout under each stage gives the settings back as an object to paste into POLE_MARKER.
