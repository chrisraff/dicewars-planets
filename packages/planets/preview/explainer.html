How the game works

The explainer the menu opens: the rules a player can lose a match without ever working out — that attacking empties the attacker, that the defender takes ties, that a one-die lead is worth far less between big stacks, and that income is paid on your largest joined-up region and then scattered where it likes. The odds figures are read out of core’s winProbability rather than typed in, so they cannot quietly stop being true.

The pictures of the planet are committed PNGs, shot on the figures page.
