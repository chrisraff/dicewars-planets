Payout tray

The chips that stand for the dice a turn just earned, peeling back one at a time as each die lands on the planet. A payout runs to MAX_RESERVE — 64 dice — so on a phone it wraps onto several lines routinely, and the order it empties in is only visible once it does. Every tray here is a real HUD draining on the real reinforceTimeline; the drain is capped at a second whatever the count, so use slowly or step to actually read one.
