Explainer figures

Where the pictures in the explainer come from. Each one is built from a pinned seed and a hand-built board, framed from a fixed camera and saved at exactly 600×400 whatever screen it was shot on — so re-taking one gives back the same picture rather than a similar one. The orbit controls are off for that reason: a shot you can nudge is a shot you cannot take twice.

Save them, then put the files in packages/planets/public/explainer/ and commit them. Until they are there the explainer draws a dashed box naming the shot that is missing, so it works before they exist and says so. These go stale silently when the renderer or the generator moves — that is what one press of Save all is for.
