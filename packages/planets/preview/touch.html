Press, drag, and who owns it

Two things want every press on the planet: tapping a territory, and turning the planet. They used to run side by side — the camera moved from the first pixel, and whether the press had been a tap was worked out afterwards, once it was too late to say anything about it. Now a press is owned: tapping gets first refusal and holds it until the press has moved far enough to plainly be a drag, at which point it is handed on for good. What that buys is the mark below — the territory under your finger lights up while there is still time to choose, so releasing is a confirmation rather than a guess.
