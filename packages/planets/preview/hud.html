HUD & player stats

Every stats-row state, using the same createHud and the same hud.css the game does — stats come from the real playerStatsFor, so nothing here can drift from what you see in a match. Reserve badges are the small +n in the lower-right of each tile; tiles with nothing banked show no badge at all and must be exactly the same size as ones that do. The battle readout sits under the stats row — tap one to open its history.
