Throwing, cancelling, skipping

A real board that refills itself, so an attack can be thrown as many times as it takes to judge one. The throw is the hardest thing in the game to catch in a match — it lasts two seconds, it needs a fight to happen at all, and the interesting half of it is over in under one — so the numbers that shape it are on sliders beside it.

Tap one of your territories and then a neighbour, exactly as in the game. While the × is up, a tap anywhere calls the attack off and the toast says so; dragging turns the planet and never cancels. Once it has gone, a tap on a territory you are about to own skips the rest of the throw and picks it up, so you never wait out a move you have finished making.
